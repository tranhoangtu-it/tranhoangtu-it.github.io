### Part 1: Early-Stage Capital ($1K – $500K | Pre-Seed & Non-Dilutive)

At the earliest stage, the biggest mistake founders make is giving up 15–20% equity/tokens before shipping a working devnet or mainnet prototype. Solana's bottom-up funnel lets you validate product-market fit with **0% dilution** first.

#### 1. Superteam Instagrants & Regional Chapter Grants ($1,000 – $10,000 | 0% Dilution)
- **Typical Check Size:** `$1,000 – $10,000 USDC`
- **Terms & Conditions:** **100% non-dilutive** (no equity, no token warrants). Disbursed either upfront or across 2–3 shipping milestones (working repo, mainnet deployment, initial user cohort). Turnaround time on initial decisions is typically **72 hours to 7 days**.
- **Who Qualifies:** Solo builders and 2–4 person teams building open-source tooling, consumer apps, DePIN utilities, PayFi apps, or localized ecosystem growth initiatives.
- **How Regional Chapters Multiply Odds:** Applying through regional chapters such as **@SuperteamUKR**, Superteam Germany, Superteam Vietnam, or Superteam UK gives founders direct mentor office hours, pitch refinement, and local builder feedback before capital deployment. For example, **OOBE Protocol (Steve Capital)** leveraged a Superteam regional grant + ideathon win to ship an autonomous agent runtime integrating 400+ Solana tools.

#### 2. Solana Foundation Milestone Grants & Convertible Grants ($10,000 – $250,000)
- **Typical Check Size:**
  - **Standard Public-Goods Grants:** `$10,000 – $100,000` (non-dilutive; focused on open-source SDKs, developer infrastructure, ZK compression, validator diversity, and security tooling).
  - **Convertible Grants:** `$50,000 – $250,000+` for commercial for-profit protocols building strategic Solana primitives.
- **Terms & Conditions:**
  - Standard grants require **open-source MIT/Apache-2.0 deliverables** and strict milestone verification (KYC/AML required before payout).
  - Convertible grants remain non-dilutive unless the project subsequently raises a priced institutional equity/token round above an agreed threshold—at which point the grant converts on standard SAFE + Token Warrant terms.

#### 3. Colosseum Hackathon + Accelerator ($250,000 Pre-Seed | Fixed Standard Terms)
- **Typical Check Size:**
  - **Hackathon Track Prizes:** `$10,000 – $50,000 USDC` (non-dilutive prize money across DeFi, Consumer, DePIN, Payments, and Gaming tracks).
  - **Colosseum Accelerator Investment:** **$250,000** direct pre-seed investment for the ~10–15 teams accepted out of each global Solana hackathon cohort.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Standardized founder-friendly YC-style pre-seed SAFE + token side letter (~7% ownership benchmark), paired with a 6-week private sprint, direct intros to top Solana angels (Anatoly Yakovenko, Raj Gokal, Mert Helius, Jupiter/Jito founders), and Demo Day access to tier-1 crypto VCs.
- **Proven Precedents:** Solana category leaders—including **Tensor**, **StepN**, **Squads**, **Ore**, and **MagicBlock** ecosystem alumni—used global Solana hackathons as their primary launchpad into seed rounds.

### Part 2: Mid-Stage Capital ($500K – $25M+ | Post-Traction & Growth)

Once a protocol demonstrates verifiable onchain traction—daily active wallets, retained fee revenue, TVL velocity, or B2B API volume—founders unlock four distinct mid-stage instruments.

#### 4. Curated Angel Syndicates & Merit-Based Community Rounds (Echo, Legion, Buidlpad | $300K – $3M)
- **Typical Check Size:** `$300,000 – $3,000,000` aggregated from operator-angels and verified onchain power users.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Same valuation cap and unlock schedule as lead seed VCs (often `$15M – $50M FDV`, 12-month cliff or KPI-based unlocks), or shorter liquid unlock windows on merit-scored platforms like **Legion** and **Echo**.
- **Strategic Advantage:** Instead of passive balance-sheet capital, your cap table gains 200–1,000 active Solana developers, validators, and power users who actually route volume through your protocol.

#### 5. Futarchy & Onchain Market-Governed Raises (MetaDAO Launchpad | $500K – $5M+)
- **Typical Check Size:** `$500,000 – $5,000,000+ USDC` committed directly into onchain treasuries.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Pioneered natively on Solana via **MetaDAO**, decision markets ("futarchy") govern treasury spending and founder Allowances. Investors receive liquid tokens backed by a treasury protected from rug-pulls: if a team fails to execute, conditional pass/fail markets allow token holders to propose treasury redemptions or leadership changes.
- **Proven Precedents:** Solana-native teams like **mtnCapital**, **Future**, and ** Dean's List / MetaDAO ecosystem projects** proved that transparent onchain governance eliminates multi-month legal roadshows while aligning tokenholders and builders.

#### 6. Seed & Series A Institutional Venture Rounds ($2M – $25M+)
- **Typical Check Size:**
  - **Seed:** `$2,000,000 – $6,000,000` at `$20M – $50M` post-money valuation / FDV (typically 10%–15% dilution via SAFE + Token Warrant or SAFT).
  - **Series A:** `$8,000,000 – $25,000,000+` at `$60M – $200M+` valuation once annualized protocol revenue or dominant category share is established.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Institutional lead VCs (Multicoin Capital, Colosseum Ventures, Solana Ventures, Framework, Polychain, Pantera, Frictionless, 6th Man Ventures, Delphi Ventures) typically require a **1-year cliff + 24 to 36-month linear vesting** for both team and investor tokens, audited Anchor/Rust programs, and clear legal structuring (Cayman/BVI foundation + DevCo).
- **Verified Solana Fundraising Benchmarks:**
  - **Backpack (Coral):** Raised **$20M** strategic round followed by **$17M Series A at a $120M valuation** (led by Placeholder VC) after proving execution with Mad Lads, xNFTs, and regulated exchange infrastructure.
  - **Drift Protocol:** Raised **$3.8M Seed** (led by Multicoin Capital) -> scaled perpetuals volume past billions -> closed **$23.5M Series B** (led by Polychain Capital) to expand Solana DeFi derivatives.
  - **Squads Labs:** Raised **$5.7M Strategic** -> **$10M Series A** (led by Electric Capital) after becoming the multisig and smart-account standard securing billions in Solana treasury value.
  - **MagicBlock:** Raised **$3M Pre-Seed** + **$7.5M Seed** (led by Lightspeed Faction) after proving ephemeral rollups for sub-50ms real-time Solana gaming and high-frequency apps.
  - **Jito Labs:** Raised **$10M Series A** (led by Multicoin & Framework) after building client infrastructure that captured the majority of Solana MEV-enabled stake.
